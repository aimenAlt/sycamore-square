        TERMS EXPLAINED

        What the technical words mean

        SF, ac
        Square feet and acres. One acre is 43,560 SF.
        Zoning, CMU-3
        Zoning decides which uses are allowed on a lot. CMU-3 is the Memphis commercial mixed-use district these parcels are in.
        100-year floodplain, FEMA Zone AE
        Land with a 1% chance of flooding in any year, as mapped by FEMA. Federally backed loans there require flood insurance.
        50% rule (substantial improvement)
        If the work costs 50% or more of the building’s value, the whole building must meet current flood rules.
        Elevation certificate, LOMA
        A surveyor’s measurement of the building’s height against the flood level. If it sits above it, FEMA can issue a Letter of Map Amendment (LOMA) taking it out of the flood zone.
        Due diligence
        The checks a buyer makes before closing, such as survey, title, environmental and zoning.
        ALTA survey
        A detailed boundary survey of the kind title companies and lenders rely on.
        Phase I
        An environmental review of records and the site, looking for past contamination.
        NNN (triple net) lease
        The tenant pays rent plus its share of property tax, insurance and common-area costs.
        Net operating income (NOI)
        Yearly rent after the property’s running costs, before loan payments.
        Yield on cost
        Net operating income divided by the total project cost. Higher is better.
        Cap rate
        The rate buyers use to turn yearly income into a price: value = income ÷ cap rate.
        IRR (levered)
        The yearly return on the owner’s own cash over the holding period, counting the loan.
        Hard cost, soft cost
        Building work, versus design, permits, fees and financing.
        C-store, QSR
        A convenience store, and a quick-service (fast-food) restaurant.
        DXF, GLB, BLEND
        Technical file types: a CAD drawing, a 3D model and a Blender scene. They need special software to open.
